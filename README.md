# Marvel's Avengers

> "Avengers, assemble!" - *Captain America*

**The Avengers** is a superhero franchise from Marvel Studios, featuring iconic characters like Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Hawkeye, and Black Widow. It spans multiple films within the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), culminating in epic battles and emotional arcs.

## Key Films

| Title                    | Year | Highlight                              |
|--------------------------|------|----------------------------------------|
| The Avengers             | 2012 | Heroes unite to stop Loki              |
| Age of Ultron            | 2015 | AI villain threatens global extinction |
| Infinity War             | 2018 | Thanos collects Infinity Stones        |
| Endgame                  | 2019 | Time travel to undo the Snap           |

## Main Characters

- **Iron Man** – Genius billionaire in a powered suit  
- **Captain America** – Super-soldier with a moral compass  
- **Thor** – Norse god wielding Mjölnir  
- **Black Widow** – Spy and martial artist  
- **Hulk** – Scientist with a monstrous alter ego  
- **Hawkeye** – Master archer and tactician

## Why I Love It

- Epic scale and emotional depth  
- Character-driven storytelling  
- Stunning visuals and action sequences  
- Global cultural impact
